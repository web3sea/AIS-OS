# About the Business

Automation Architecture AI sells two ways: three products for solo founders, and
custom automation solutions delivered as consulting.

## Products

- **AIOS Coffee** - daily briefing and meeting prep: what is on today, what you
  need to know before each meeting, and a draft reply ready to send.
  Positioning: "Walk into every meeting prepared."
- **AIOS Signals** - self-serve relationship intelligence: import your contacts,
  see your relationship map, and get three ranked next actions. Positioning:
  "Know which relationships need you today."
- **AIOS IQ** - positioning: "The memory your AI actually trusts." Full
  description still to be written.

Coffee is the wedge product; the plan is for adoption to spill over into IQ and
Signals.

## ICP

Solo founders for the products. Ambitious companies implementing AI for the
custom automation work.

## Where revenue lands

- Products: Stripe. Rail is chosen, products are not launched yet.
- Consulting and custom automation: invoices land in Wise bank.
- Tracking: nothing consolidated today. Xero is planned for accounting, plus a

Open item: what a custom automation engagement looks like in practice (project
